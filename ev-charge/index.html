How this is calculated

Energy needed = Battery kWh × (Target % − Current %)

Units from meter = Energy needed ÷ Charging efficiency

  • Some energy is lost as heat in the charger, cable and battery. AC home charging typically loses 10–15%; DC fast charging loses about 5–8% (the station bills you for what it delivers).
  • Battery sizes are manufacturer-quoted figures; usable capacity can be a little lower, and an older battery holds less.
  • Time assumes the charger runs at full power. On DC, charging slows sharply above ~80%, so the last 20% is estimated at about one-third speed. On AC, your car's onboard charger may cap the speed.
  • Cold or very hot weather, battery conditioning and the AC running during charging all add a few units.
